DRAWING UP AND REVIEWING A BEST AVAILABLE TECHNIQUES (BAT) REFERENCE DOCUMENT 1.1 Context 1.1.1 Legislative context In 2024, Directive (EU) 2024/1785 ( 1 ) of the Parliament and the Council amended Directive 2010/75/EU ( 2 ) of the Parliament and the Council (‘the revised IED’), which lays down rules on integrated prevention and control of pollution arising from industrial activities. It also lays down rules designed to prevent or, where that is not practicable, to reduce continuously emissions into air, water and land, prevent the generation of waste, improve resource efficiency, and promote the circular economy and decarbonisation, in order to achieve a high level of protection of human health and the environment taken as a whole (see Article 1 of the revised IED). Article 13(1) of the revised IED, requires that the Commission organise an exchange of information between it and Member States, the industries concerned, non-governmental organisations promoting human health or environmental protection and the European Chemicals Agency in order to draw up, review and, where necessary, update BAT reference documents (‘BREFs’). The Commission should also ensure that the European Environment Agency participates in the exchange of information, where the exchange of information would benefit from the Agency's expertise ( 3 ) . Article 13(1) also sets out ‘that exchange of information shall aim at an eight-year review cycle of BAT reference documents prioritising the documents that have the highest potential to improve the protection of human health or the environment.
