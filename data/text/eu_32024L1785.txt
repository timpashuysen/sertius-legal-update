It also aims to ensure that such transition is just and inclusive, leaving no one behind. The Union is also committed to the Paris Agreement ( 4 ) , the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development and its Sustainable Development Goals as well as to its involvement in the World Health Organization. The Union’s Chemicals Strategy for Sustainability of October 2020 and the Zero Pollution Action Plan adopted in May 2021 specifically address pollution aspects of the European Green Deal.
