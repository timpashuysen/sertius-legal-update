HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION: Article 1 Administrative information 1. For the purpose of reporting administrative information to the Commission pursuant to Article 7(1) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1244, Member States shall use the format set out in Annex I to this Decision and the harmonised electronic reporting tool made available by the Commission. 2. The administrative information set out in Annex I shall be submitted to the Commission by 30 September of the year following the reporting year. 3. For installations falling within the scope of Directive 2010/75/EU, Member States shall report the information set out in Annex I only to the extent that the same information has not already been reported in accordance with Article 72 of that Directive. Article 2 Thematic information 1. For the purpose of reporting thematic information to the Commission, pursuant to Article 7(1) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1244, Member States shall use the format set out in Annex II to this Decision and the harmonised electronic reporting tool made available by the Commission. 2. The thematic information set out in Annexes II, IV, V, VI and VII shall be submitted to the Commission by 30 November of the year following the reporting year. Article 3 Nomenclature of activities For the purpose of reporting the activities listed in Annex I to Regulation (EU) 2024/1244, Member States shall use the nomenclature set out in Annex III. Article 4 First reporting years 1. The information set out in Annexes I, II, IV, VI and VII shall be first submitted for the reporting year 2027. 2. The information set out in Annex V shall be first submitted for the reporting year 2028. 3. By way of derogation from paragraph 1, for installations rearing poultry and pigs which were not included within the scope of Directive 2010/75/EU in the version in force on 3 August 2024, the first reporting year shall be: (a) 2030 for installations with a capacity of 600 livestock units (LSU) or more; (b) 2031 for installations with a capacity of 400 livestock units (LSU) or more; (c) 2032 for all other installations rearing poultry and pigs within the scope of Regulation (EU) 2024/1244 set out in Annex I to that Regulation. Article 5 Prefilled templates 1. From the reporting year 2028, the Commission assisted by the European Environment Agency, shall make available to the Member States prefilled electronic reporting templates for the information set out in Annex I, through the harmonised electronic reporting tool. Those templates shall be based on the data reported by Member States for the reporting year 2027. In subsequent years, the prefilling will be based on the most recent information validated or otherwise reported by Member States. From the reporting year 2028, Member States may choose one of the following methods: (a) to verify and, if necessary, update the prefilled templates and submit them to the Commission; (b) to complete the templates without using the prefilled version. 2. From the reporting year 2027, the Commission assisted by the European Environment Agency, shall allocate each installation to a River Basin District based on the reported coordinates of the installation and the most recent valid River Basin District codes in the Water Information System for Europe (‘WISE’). Article 6 Below-threshold reporting and off-site transfers of waste 1. In accordance with Article 6(2) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1244, where any of the thresholds set out in Article 6(1), points (a), (b) or (c), of that Regulation are not exceeded for any installation in their territory, Member States shall, in relation to those installations, in addition to administrative data set out in Annex I, report that the release of pollutants or the off-site transfers of pollutants or waste are below the applicable reporting thresholds for the first reporting year. 2. Member States may decide to report on the below-threshold status every year or to report it only the first time the below-threshold status is observed. 3. In Member States where the national reporting system does not prescribe reporting thresholds for operators to fulfil the requirements of Article 6 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1244, any reporting of release or transfer values that are below the thresholds set out in Annex II to Regulation (EU) 2024/1244 or Article 6(1), point (b) of that Regulation, shall be regarded as equivalent to a declaration that the releases or transfers are below the applicable thresholds for the purposes of Article 6(2) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1244. In such cases, operators shall not be required to submit a separate declaration of below-threshold status to their competent authority, as the information provided under the national system is deemed to fulfil the requirement of Article 6(2) of that Regulation. 4. For the purposes of reporting under Article 6(1), point (b), of Regulation (EU) 2024/1244, Member States shall ensure that operators report off-site transfers of waste only when such waste leaves the boundaries of the facility. Article 7 Confidentiality In accordance with Article 11 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1244, Member States shall indicate for each facility, installation and part of an installation, which data cannot be made public and provide the reasons for that. In such cases, only the specific data elements identified as confidential by the Member State, will not be made public through the Industrial Emissions Portal. This shall not affect the publication of other data for the same facility, installation or a part of an installation. Article 8 Repeal Implementing Decision (EU) 2019/1741 is repealed with effect from 31 December 2027. Article 9 Application This Decision shall apply from 1 January 2028. Article 10 Addressees This Decision is addressed to the Member States. Done at Brussels, 13 October 2026.
