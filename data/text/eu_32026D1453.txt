L_202601453EN.000101.fmx.xml Official Journal of the European Union EN L series 2026/1453 10.7.2026 COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING DECISION (EU) 2026/1453 of 2 July 2026 correcting Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2024/411 on the list of shipping companies specifying the administering authority in respect of a shipping company in accordance with Directive 2003/87/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council (Text with EEA relevance) THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION, Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, Having regard to Directive 2003/87/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 October 2003 establishing a system for greenhouse gas emission allowance trading within the Union and amending Council Directive 96/61/EC ( 1 ) , and in particular Article 3gf(2), point (b), thereof, Whereas: (1) The Annex to Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2024/411 ( 2 ) does not accurately reflect attributions of four shipping companies nor changes to the IMO numbers of 21 shipping companies recorded in Thetis-MRV before 11 August 2025. To ensure consistency in the format of the data, entries recorded in a manner deviating from the IMO identification scheme should be left blank. (2) Implementing Decision (EU) 2024/411 should, therefore, be corrected to reflect those reattributions and the corresponding updates to the IMO numbers of the shipping companies, HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION: Article 1 The Annex to Implementing Decision (EU) 2024/411 is replaced by the text set out in the Annex to this Decision. Article 2 This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union . Done at Brussels, 2 July 2026.
