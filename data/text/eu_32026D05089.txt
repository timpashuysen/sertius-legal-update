C_202605089EN.000101.fmx.xml Official Journal of the European Union EN C series C/2026/5089 5.10.2026 COMMISSION DECISION of 3 August 2026 instructing the Central Administrator of the Union Registry to enter the national allocation tables of Belgium, Bulgaria, Czechia, Denmark, Germany, Estonia, Ireland, Greece, Spain, France, Croatia, Italy, Cyprus, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Hungary, the Netherlands, Austria, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovenia, Slovakia, Finland and Sweden into the Union Registry (C/2026/5089) THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION, Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, Having regard to Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/1122 of 12 March 2019 supplementing Directive 2003/87/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the functioning of the Union Registry ( 1 ) , and in particular Article 46(2) thereof, Whereas: (1) Member States submitted to the Commission the list of installations in their territory pursuant to Article 11(1) of Directive 2003/87/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council ( 2 ) . (2) In accordance with Commission Decision (EU) 2026/1312 ( 3 ) the Commission raised no objections to the list of installations covered by Directive 2003/87/EC as submitted by Belgium, Bulgaria, Czechia, Denmark, Germany, Estonia, Ireland, Greece, Spain, France, Croatia, Italy, Cyprus, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Hungary, the Netherlands, Austria, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovenia, Slovakia, Finland and Sweden, except as set out in the second subparagraph of the Article 1 and in the Annex to that Decision. (3) Member States and EEA EFTA States determined and notified the preliminary annual amounts of free allowances per installation using the revised benchmark values determined in Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1412 ( 4 ) pursuant to Article 14(5) of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/331 ( 5 ) . (4) In Article 1 of Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1862 ( 6 ) , it was determined that no adjustment needs to be applied to the preliminary annual amounts of free allowances pursuant to Article 10a(5) of Directive (EU) 2003/87, as demand did not exceed the maximum amount. (5) By letter dated 24 July 2026, Belgium notified its national allocation table for the transitional free allocation of emission allowances for the period from 2026 to 2030 with the final annual amounts of emission allowances drawn up on the basis of Article 46(1) of Delegated Regulation (EU) No 2019/1122.
