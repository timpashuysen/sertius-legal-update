TITLE: Corrigendum to Commission Regulation (EU) 2023/2055 of 25 September 2023 amending Annex XVII to Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006 of the European Parliament and of the Council concerning the Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals (REACH) as regards synthetic polymer microparticles (OJ L 238, 27.9.2023)

L_202590479EN.000101.fmx.xml Official Journal of the European Union EN L series 2025/90479 6.6.2025 Corrigendum to Commission Regulation (EU) 2023/2055 of 25 September 2023 amending Annex XVII to Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006 of the European Parliament and of the Council concerning the Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals (REACH) as regards synthetic polymer microparticles ( Official Journal of the European Union L 238 of 27 September 2023 ) On page 77, in paragraph 1 of column 2 of the table in the Annex, amending Annex XVII to Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006: for: ‘Shall not be placed on the market as substances on their own or, where the synthetic polymer microparticles are present to confer a sought-after characteristic, in mixtures in a concentration equal to or greater than 0,01 % by weight.’, read: ‘Shall not be placed on the market on their own or, where the synthetic polymer microparticles are present to confer a sought-after characteristic, in mixtures in a concentration equal to or greater than 0,01 % by weight.’. On page 78, in paragraph 4(a) of column 2 of the table in the Annex, amending Annex XVII to Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006: for: ‘synthetic polymer microparticles, as substances on their own or in mixtures, for use at industrial sites.’, read: ‘synthetic polymer microparticles on their own or in mixtures, for use at industrial sites.’. On page 78, in the first sub-paragraph of paragraph 5 of column 2 of the table in the Annex, amending Annex XVII to Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006: for: ‘Paragraph 1 shall not apply to the placing on the market of the following synthetic polymer microparticles, as substances on their own or in mixtures:’, read: ‘Paragraph 1 shall not apply to the placing on the market of the following synthetic polymer microparticles on their own or in mixtures:’. On page 79, in paragraph 7(b) of column 2 of the table in the Annex, amending Annex XVII to Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006: for: ‘The synthetic polymer microparticles supplied is subject to conditions laid down by entry 78 of Annex XVII to Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006 of the European Parliament and of the Council.’, read: ‘The synthetic polymer microparticles supplied are subject to conditions laid down by entry 78 of Annex XVII to Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006 of the European Parliament and of the Council.’.
