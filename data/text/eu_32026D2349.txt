TITLE: Commission Decision (EU) 2026/2349 of 9 October 2026 recognising certain critical raw material projects as Strategic Projects under Regulation (EU) 2024/1252 of the European Parliament and of the Council (notified under document C(2026) 8051)

L_202602349EN.000101.fmx.xml Official Journal of the European Union EN L series 2026/2349 22.10.2026 COMMISSION DECISION (EU) 2026/2349 of 9 October 2026 recognising certain critical raw material projects as Strategic Projects under Regulation (EU) 2024/1252 of the European Parliament and of the Council (notified under document C(2026) 8051) (Only the English text is authentic) THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION, Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2024/1252 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 April 2024 establishing a framework for ensuring a secure and sustainable supply of critical raw materials and amending Regulations (EU) No 168/2013, (EU) 2018/858, (EU) 2018/1724 and (EU) 2019/1020 ( 1 ) , and in particular Article 7(9) thereof, Whereas: (1) Pursuant to Article 1(2), point (a) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1252 and in view of securing the Union’s supply of critical raw materials, the Commission is to identify projects for the extraction, processing, recycling or substitution of strategic raw materials, with the aim of recognising such projects as Strategic Projects under the framework laid down in Article 7 of that Regulation. (2) In accordance with Article 7(3) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1252, the Commission launched on 23 May 2024 a call to apply for the recognition of a critical raw material project as a Strategic Project ( 2 ) , with a second cut-off date on 15 January 2026. (3) Following the submission of applications, the Commission informed the applicants on 13 February 2026 whether the information provided was considered complete in accordance with Article 7(4) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1252. (4) At the time of the second cut-off date on 15 January 2026, 101 applications were submitted from proposed projects located in Member States, 98 of which the Commission considered complete in accordance with Article 7(1) and (4) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1252. (5) In accordance with Article 7(5) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1252, the Commission informed the European Critical Raw Materials Board (‘the Board’) established by Article 35(1) of that Regulation of the applications the Commission considered complete. (6) Thereafter, the Commission transmitted the full applications, in accordance with Article 7(7) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1252, to Member States whose territory is concerned by the proposed project or projects. The Member States concerned had the opportunity to provide technical feedback and object to those proposed projects in accordance with Article 7(8) of that Regulation. (7) The Commission assessed those applications in accordance with the criteria specified in Article 6(1) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1252, with the support of external experts with professional expertise in the technical, financial, environmental, social and governance (ESG) dimensions of a project, as well as in the United Nations Framework Classification for Resources (UNFC) ( 3 ) . The expert report provided the basis for the Commission’s assessment. (8) In particular, the Commission assessed those applications in light of their contribution to the security of the Union’s supply of strategic raw materials, technical feasibility, sustainable implementation and cross-border benefits, pursuant to Article 6(1) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1252. The projects that fulfil all the criteria set out in that Article should therefore be recognised as Strategic Projects. (9) The Commission provided the Board with its assessment in accordance with Article 7(6), second subparagraph, of Regulation (EU) 2024/1252. None of the projects that fulfil all the criteria provided for in Article 6(1) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1252 listed in the Annex to this Decision received an objection from the Member States whose territory is concerned by the proposed projects in accordance with Article 7(8) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1252. (10) The Commission should therefore notify all applicants for the recognition of a project as Strategic Project. In particular, the Commission should notify all applicants of the result of its assessment and the reasoning thereof ensuring the protection of trade and business secrets and other sensitive, confidential and classified information, in accordance with Article 46 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1252. (11) The Commission is to provide this Decision to the Board and to the Member State or Member States whose territory is concerned by the project or projects, in accordance with Article 7(9), second subparagraph, of Regulation (EU) 2024/1252. (12) This Decision is in accordance with the opinion of the Board, HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION: Article 1 The projects listed in the Annex are recognised as Strategic Projects.
