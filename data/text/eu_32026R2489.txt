L_202602489EN.000101.fmx.xml Official Journal of the European Union EN L series 2026/2489 11.11.2026 COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING REGULATION (EU) 2026/2489 of 10 November 2026 on granting a time-limited derogation, pursuant to Article 9(4) of Regulation (EU) 2024/590 of the European Parliament and of the Council on substances that deplete the ozone layer, from the end date for the critical use of portable fire extinguishers containing halon-1211 or halon-2402 for the protection of cabins and crew compartments in non-EU aircraft (Text with EEA relevance) THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION, Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2024/590 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 7 February 2024 on substances that deplete the ozone layer and repealing Regulation (EC) No 1005/2009 ( 1 ) , and in particular Article 9(4) thereof, Whereas: (1) Regulation (EU) 2024/590 permits the critical uses of halons listed in Annex V until the end dates specified therein. (2) Annex V to Regulation (EU) 2024/590 establishes 31 December 2025 as the end date for the critical use of portable fire extinguishers containing halon-1211 or halon-2402 for the protection of aircraft cabins and crew compartments. This means that their import, placing on the market, use and export is prohibited from that date. (3) Under the standards of the International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO), portable halon-based fire extinguishers may no longer be installed on aircraft for which the individual certificate of airworthiness was first issued on or after 31 December 2018. Nevertheless, aircraft for which the individual certificate of airworthiness was first issued before that date may continue to operate with such extinguishers, where permitted under the law of the State of registry. (4) Germany requested, pursuant to Article 9(4) of Regulation (EU) 2024/590, a derogation concerning aircraft registered in third countries and equipped with portable fire extinguishers containing, or whose functioning relies upon, halon-1211 or halon-2402 that temporarily enter the Union for maintenance, repair or overhaul activities and then leave the territory. (5) Having examined the specific case covered by Germany’s request, no economically feasible alternative is currently available within the meaning of Article 9(4) of Regulation (EU) 2024/590 that would allow maintenance, repair or overhaul to be carried out within the Union without permitting the temporary import into and export from the Union of the aircraft and, where necessary, of portable fire extinguishers or parts thereof. It is therefore appropriate to grant a time-limited derogation (‘the derogation’) from the end date laid down for that specific case in Annex V to that Regulation. (6) In order for the derogation to be effective, the specific case should include the temporary import into and subsequent export from the Union of the aircraft concerned and, where strictly necessary for the maintenance, repair or overhaul of such aircraft, the import into and supply within the Union of portable fire extinguishers containing, or whose functioning relies upon, halon-1211 or halon-2402, or parts thereof. (7) Without the derogation provided for in this Regulation, such aircraft would be prevented from obtaining the maintenance, repair or overhaul necessary to maintain their airworthiness in the Union where portable fire extinguishers containing, or whose functioning relies upon, halon-1211 or halon-2402 require replacement or servicing. This would effectively prevent Union maintenance, repair and overhaul providers from servicing a significant part of the existing third-country fleet that remains lawfully equipped with such fire extinguishers. (8) In order to ensure that the derogation remains proportionate, it should apply only to aircraft manufactured before 31 December 2018. (9) In accordance with Article 9(4) of Regulation (EU) 2024/590, reporting requirements are to be included in the derogation for monitoring its use. (10) Nothing in this Regulation should prevent the replacement, during maintenance, repair or overhaul, of portable fire extinguishers containing halons with suitable non-halon alternatives that comply with applicable Union and international aviation legislation. (11) The measures provided for in this Regulation are in accordance with the opinion of the Committee established by Regulation (EU) 2024/590, HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION: Article 1 Derogation 1. By way of a derogation from the end date set out in point 4.2 of the table in Annex V to Regulation (EU) 2024/590, the following activities shall be allowed until 31 December 2030: (a) the temporary import into and subsequent export from the Union, exclusively for the purposes of maintenance, repair or overhaul, of aircraft registered in third countries, for which the individual certificate of airworthiness was first issued before 31 December 2018 and equipped, where permitted under the law of the State of registry, with portable fire extinguishers containing, or whose functioning relies upon, halon-1211 or halon-2402; (b) where strictly necessary for the maintenance, repair or overhaul of aircraft referred to in point (a), the import into the Union of portable fire extinguishers containing, or whose functioning relies upon, halon-1211 or halon-2402, or parts thereof. 2. Portable fire extinguishers imported pursuant to paragraph 1, point (b), may be supplied to another undertaking only where such supply is strictly necessary for the maintenance, repair or overhaul of aircraft referred to in paragraph 1, point (a). Where those portable fire extinguishers are no longer intended for such use, the halon contained therein shall be recovered and reclaimed, where feasible, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2024/590. 3. Portable fire extinguishers imported pursuant to paragraph 1, point (b), shall bear a label prior to release for free circulation clearly indicating that they are imported exclusively for the purpose of maintenance, repair or overhaul of aircraft referred to in paragraph 1, point (a). Article 2 Record keeping and reporting 1. Member States shall ensure that undertakings benefiting from the derogation keep records demonstrating compliance with Article 2 and retain those records for at least five years after 14 November 2031. Those records shall consist of: (a) the identity of the aircraft in respect of which the derogation was applied; (b) the number of portable fire extinguishers containing halon-1211 or halon-2402, or parts thereof, imported or supplied within the Union pursuant to Article 2(b); (c) where applicable, the quantities of halon recovered and reclaimed. 2. By 30 June 2028 and by 30 June 2031 Member States shall submit to the Commission information on the application of the derogation until 31 December 2027 and from 1 January 2028 to 31 December 2030. That information shall be based on the records referred to in paragraph 1. Article 3 Enter into force This Regulation shall enter into force on the third day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union . This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States. Done at Brussels, 10 November 2026.
