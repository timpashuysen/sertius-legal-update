TITLE: Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/2252 of 12 October 2026 repealing Implementing Decision (EU) 2024/1283 postponing the expiry date of the approval of cis-tricos-9-ene for use in biocidal products of product-type 19 in accordance with Regulation (EU) No 528/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council

L_202602252EN.000101.fmx.xml Official Journal of the European Union EN L series 2026/2252 13.10.2026 COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING DECISION (EU) 2026/2252 of 12 October 2026 repealing Implementing Decision (EU) 2024/1283 postponing the expiry date of the approval of cis-tricos-9-ene for use in biocidal products of product-type 19 in accordance with Regulation (EU) No 528/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council (Text with EEA relevance) THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION, Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, Having regard to Regulation (EU) No 528/2012 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 22 May 2012 concerning the making available on the market and use of biocidal products ( 1 ) , and in particular Article 14(5) thereof, After consulting the Standing Committee on Biocidal Products, Whereas: (1) Cis-tricos-9-ene was included in Annex I to Directive 98/8/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council ( 2 ) as an active substance for use in biocidal products of product-type 19. Pursuant to Article 86 of Regulation (EU) No 528/2012, it is therefore deemed approved under that Regulation subject to the requirements set out in Annex I to Directive 98/8/EC. (2) On 6 April 2023, an application was submitted in accordance with Article 13(1) of Regulation (EU) No 528/2012 for the renewal of the approval of cis-tricos-9-ene for use in biocidal products of product-type 19 (‘the application’). The application was evaluated by the competent authority of Austria. (3) As the approval of cis-tricos-9-ene was to expire on 30 September 2024, Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2024/1283 ( 3 ) postponed that expiry date to 31 March 2027, in order to allow sufficient time for the examination of the application. (4) Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/2247 ( 4 ) renewed the approval of cis-tricos-9-ene for use in biocidal products of product-type 19, subject to the conditions in the Annex to that Regulation, including a new expiry date of approval. Therefore, it is appropriate to repeal Implementing Decision (EU) 2024/1283, HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION: Article 1 Implementing Decision (EU) 2024/1283 is repealed. Article 2 This Decision shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union . Done at Brussels, 12 October 2026.
