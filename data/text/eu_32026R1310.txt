HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION: Article 1 1. The assessment and verification system laid down in Annex I to this Regulation shall apply to the essential characteristics of specific products which are deemed to achieve a certain level or class of performance without testing or calculation, or without further testing or calculation, in accordance with a delegated act adopted pursuant to Article 5(6) of Regulation (EU) 2024/3110, provided that the deemed-to-satisfy provision set out in that delegated act is applied. 2. The assessment and verification system referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article shall prevail where the same essential characteristic is also laid down in accordance with Articles 2 and 3 of this Regulation. Where the deemed-to-satisfy option referred to in paragraph 1 is not applied, the applicable assessment and verification system shall be the one laid down in Articles 2 and 3. Article 2 1. The assessment and verification system applicable to some essential characteristics of a horizontal nature grouped in accordance with Annex X to Regulation (EU) 2024/3110 shall be the assessment and verification systems set out in Annex II to this Regulation. 2. The assessment and verification system set out in accordance with paragraph 1 of this Article shall prevail where the same essential characteristic is also laid down in accordance with Article 3. Article 3 The assessment and verification system applicable to essential characteristics and product requirements for a specific product category, taking into consideration the intended uses and any additional remark required to properly interpret the technical content, shall be the assessment and verification systems laid down in Annex III to this Regulation. Article 4 This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union . This Regulation shall be binding in its entirety and directly applicable in all Member States. Done at Brussels, 11 June 2026.
