Action for failure to fulfil obligations – Judgment of the Court establishing the failure of a Member State to fulfil its obligations – Breach of the obligation to comply with the judgment – Financial penalties – Periodic penalty payment – Calculation of the amount – Decreasing penalty payment (Art. 260(2) TFEU) (see paragraphs 113-121) Operative part The Court: 1. Declares that, by failing to take all the necessary measures to comply with the judgment of 5 September 2019, Commission v Portugal (Designation and protection of special areas of conservation) ( C‑290/18 , EU:C:2019:669 ), the Portuguese Republic failed to fulfil its obligations under Article 260(1) TFEU; 2. Orders the Portuguese Republic to pay to the European Commission: – a lump sum in the amount of EUR 10 million; – should the failure to fulfil obligations established in point 1 of the operative part persist at the date of delivery of the present judgment, a daily penalty payment in the amount of EUR 41250 from that date until the judgment of 5 September 2019, Commission v Portugal (Designation and protection of special areas of conservation) ( C‑290/18 , EU:C:2019:669 ) has been complied with in full, with a progressive reduction of EUR 750 per day for each site of Community importance that is brought into compliance with that judgment, applicable once the Commission has been provided with complete and accurate information in that regard; 3. Orders the Portuguese Republic to pay the costs.
