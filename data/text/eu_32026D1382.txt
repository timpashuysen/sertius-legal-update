L_202601382EN.000101.fmx.xml Official Journal of the European Union EN L series 2026/1382 30.6.2026 COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING DECISION (EU) 2026/1382 of 22 June 2026 determining, pursuant to Regulation (EU) 2024/573 of the European Parliament and of the Council, reference values for the period from 1 January 2027 to 31 December 2029 for each producer or importer which has lawfully placed hydrofluorocarbons on the Union market from 1 January 2022 to 31 December 2024 (notified under document C(2026) 3000) (Only the Bulgarian, Croatian, Czech, Danish, Dutch, English, Estonian, Finnish, French, German, Greek, Hungarian, Irish, Italian, Latvian, Lithuanian, Maltese, Polish, Portuguese, Romanian, Slovak, Slovenian, Spanish and Swedish texts are authentic) (Text with EEA relevance) THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION, Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2024/573 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 7 February 2024 on fluorinated greenhouse gases, amending Directive (EU) 2019/1937 and repealing Regulation (EU) No 517/2014 ( 1 ) , and in particular Article 17(1) thereof, Whereas: (1) To implement the gradual reduction of the quantities of hydrofluorocarbons that can be placed on the Union market, Regulation (EU) 2024/573 subjects their yearly placing on the Union market to quantitative limits. (2) In accordance with Article 17(1) of Regulation (EU) 2024/573, the Commission should determine reference values for each importer and producer that has lawfully placed on the Union market hydrofluorocarbons in 2022, 2023 or 2024. In accordance with point 4(a) of Annex VII to Regulation (EU) 2024/573, for the determination of reference values for 2027, 2028 and 2029, the data as reported by producers and importers in accordance with Article 19 of Regulation (EU) No 517/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council ( 2 ) for 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022 and 2023 as well as in accordance with Article 26 of Regulation (EU) 2024/573 for 2024 should be taken into account. (3) For the purpose of identifying the starting year for the annual average calculation for the reference values referred to in point 4(a) of Annex VII to Regulation (EU) 2024/573, only the period starting with the year for which quota had been allocated for the first time in accordance with Regulation (EU) No 517/2014 or Regulation (EU) 2024/573 should be taken into account, or only the period starting with the year in which the undertaking lawfully placed hydrofluorocarbons on the Union market for the first time as reported under Article 19 of Regulation (EU) No 517/2014 or Article 26 of Regulation (EU) 2024/573, whichever event occurred first. This rule should not apply to undertakings that have placed hydrofluorocarbons on the Union market on the basis of quota that included quota transferred to them in accordance with Article 18 of Regulation (EU) No 517/2014 or Article 21 of Regulation (EU) 2024/573. Instead, first, the rule should be that for each transfer in a given year, the starting year for the annual average should be the year in which the undertaking transferring their quota to the undertaking receiving the quota has entered the Union market (namely the year for which the undertaking making the transfer was allocated quota for the first time or the year in which that undertaking lawfully placed hydrofluorocarbons on the Union market for the first time, whichever event occurred first). That starting year may be 2015 or later depending on each case. Secondly, that rule should only be applied to the part of the placing on the Union market relating to quota transferred to the concerned undertaking. The part of the placing on the Union market relating to quota allocated to the concerned undertaking in a given year in accordance with Article 16(1) of Regulation (EU) No 517/2014 or 17(4) of Regulation (EU) 2024/573 should fall under the general rule. (4) In addition, in accordance with point 4(b) of Annex VII to Regulation (EU) 2024/573, a reference value is to be calculated for each producer and importer that has reported on the placing on the Union market of hydrofluorocarbons for being used for the production of metered dose inhalers for the delivery of pharmaceutical ingredients. That reference value is to be calculated on the basis of the annual average of the quantities of hydrofluorocarbons lawfully placed on the Union market for such usage, from 1 January 2020 as reported under Article 19 of Regulation (EU) No 517/2014 and under Article 26 of Regulation (EU) 2024/573, for the years available, on the basis of available data. Accordingly, for calculating the reference values referred to in point 4(b) of Annex VII to Regulation (EU) 2024/573 for the period from 1 January 2027 to 31 December 2029, any data reported by producers and importers in accordance with Article 19 of Regulation (EU) No 517/2014 for 2020, 2021, 2022 and 2023 or in accordance with Article 26 of Regulation (EU) 2024/573 for 2024 should be taken into account. (5) For the purpose of identifying the starting year for the annual average calculation for the reference values referred to in point 4(b) of Annex VII to Regulation (EU) 2024/573, only the period starting with the year for which quantities for the placing on the Union market of hydrofluorocarbons being used for the production of metered dose inhalers for the delivery of pharmaceutical ingredients were reported for the first time in accordance with Article 19 of Regulation (EU) No 517/2014 should be taken into account. (6) In accordance with Article 18(3) of Regulation (EU) 2024/573, for the purpose of determining a reference value for each importer or producer pursuant to Article 17(1) of that Regulation and allocating quota for placing hydrofluorocarbons on the Union market pursuant to Article 17(4) of that Regulation, all undertakings with the same beneficial owner(s) should be considered as one single undertaking. For this purpose, the sum of the individual average of quantities of hydrofluorocarbons lawfully placed on the Union market, for each undertaking of the same beneficial owner(s), should be taken into account when establishing a reference value of the importer or producer considered as the single undertaking of the same beneficial owner(s). Where a beneficial owner has indicated a single undertaking to the Commission other than the undertaking that was registered first in the F-gas Portal in accordance with Article 18(3) of Regulation (EU) 2024/573, in view of ensuring a fair outcome where those two undertakings placed hydrofluorocarbons on the market for the first time in different years, the starting year for the annual average calculation for the reference value related to quantities lawfully placed on the market by the indicated undertaking from the year it became the single undertaking should be the year the undertaking that was registered first in the F-gas Portal had been allocated quota for the first time in accordance with Regulation (EU) No 517/2014 or Regulation (EU) 2024/573, or the year in which that undertaking lawfully placed hydrofluorocarbons on the Union market for the first time as reported under Article 19 of Regulation (EU) No 517/2014 or Article 26 of Regulation (EU) 2024/573, whichever event occurred first. (7) Where a producer or an importer has notified the Commission in accordance with Article 17(2) of Regulation (EU) 2024/573 of a legal succession, a reference value should be determined only for those producers or importers that have been determined as the legal successors and fulfil the conditions set out in Article 18(3) of that Regulation. In view of ensuring a fair outcome where an undertaking (the “predecessor undertaking”) has been taken over by a legal successor and where each undertaking placed hydrofluorocarbons on the market for the first time in different years, the reference value for the legal successor should be determined as the sum of three parts, each reflecting average quantities placed on the market over time. The first part should reflect the average annual quantities placed on the market by the predecessor undertaking prior to the legal succession, calculated from the first year in which that undertaking lawfully placed hydrofluorocarbons on the market until 2024. The second part should reflect the average annual quantities placed on the market by the legal successor prior to the legal succession, calculated from the first year in which it lawfully placed hydrofluorocarbons on the market until 2024. The third part, should reflect the quantities placed on the market after the legal succession, taking account of the two years in which the two undertakings respectively commenced lawfully placing hydrofluorocarbons on the market.
