That assessment states, on several occasions and in particular in Section 2, entitled ‘Problem definition’, and in Section 6.2.1.1.5 thereof, entitled ‘Innovation and competitiveness’ (see, in particular, Part 1, pp. 14, 15, 40 and 41), that the only way for the European Union to remain a leader and competitive in the world market is to increase in its territory the production of such vehicles and the batteries necessary for them, as well as research and development in that area. Furthermore, contrary to the Republic of Poland’s submissions, according to the impact assessment, the automotive industry itself acknowledges that the EU legislative framework will help it to maintain and develop its market share in the European Union and worldwide. 156 In the light of the foregoing considerations, the fourth plea in law must be rejected as unfounded and, consequently, the action must be dismissed in its entirety. Costs 157 Under Article 138(1) of the Rules of Procedure of the Court of Justice, the unsuccessful party is to be ordered to pay the costs if they have been applied for in the successful party’s pleadings. Since the Parliament and the Council have applied for costs to be awarded against the Republic of Poland, and since the latter has been unsuccessful, the Republic of Poland must be ordered to bear its own costs and to pay those incurred by the Parliament and the Council. 158 In accordance with Article 140(1) of those rules, pursuant to which the institutions which have intervened in the proceedings are to bear their own costs, the Commission shall bear its own costs. On those grounds, the Court (Second Chamber) hereby: 1. Dismisses the action; 2.
