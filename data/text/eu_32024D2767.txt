L_202402767EN.000101.fmx.xml Official Journal of the European Union EN L series 2024/2767 15.11.2024 COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING DECISION (EU) 2024/2767 of 30 October 2024 determining, pursuant to Regulation (EU) 2024/573 of the European Parliament and of the Council, reference values for the period from 1 January 2025 to 31 December 2026 for each producer or importer which has lawfully placed hydrofluorocarbons on the Union market from 1 January 2021 to 31 December 2023 (notified under document C(2024) 8500) (Only the Bulgarian, Czech, Croatian, Danish, Dutch, English, Estonian, Finnish, French, Irish, German, Greek, Hungarian, Italian, Latvian, Lithuanian, Maltese, Polish, Portuguese, Romanian, Slovakian, Slovenian, Spanish and Swedish texts are authentic) (Text with EEA relevance) THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION, Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2024/573 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 7 February 2024 on fluorinated greenhouse gases, amending Directive (EU) 2019/1937 and repealing Regulation (EU) No 517/2014 ( 1 ) , and in particular Article 17(1) thereof, Whereas: (1) To implement the gradual reduction of the quantities of hydrofluorocarbons that can be placed on the Union market, Regulation (EU) 2024/573 subjects their yearly placing on the market to quantitative limits. (2) Pursuant to Article 17(1) of Regulation (EU) 2024/573, the Commission should determine reference values for each importer and producer that has lawfully placed on the Union market hydrofluorocarbons (HFCs) in 2021, 2022 or 2023, in accordance with the calculation mechanism referred to in points 4(a) and 4(b) of Annex VII to Regulation (EU) 2024/573. (3) As established by Annex VII to Regulation (EU) 2024/573, the maximum quantities of HFCs allowed to be placed on the Union market in 2025 and 2026 should be the same and are to decrease as from 2027. In this regard, it is appropriate that this Decision establishes reference values for 2025 and 2026. (4) In accordance with point 4(a) of Annex VII to Regulation (EU) 2024/573, for the determination of reference values for 2025 and 2026, the data reported by producers and importers in accordance with Article 19 of Regulation (EU) No 517/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council ( 2 ) for 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022 and 2023 should be taken into account. (5) For the purpose of identifying the starting year for the annual average calculation for the reference values referred to in point 4(a) of Annex VII to Regulation (EU) 2024/573, only the period starting with the year for which quota had been allocated for the first time in accordance with Article 17(1) of Regulation (EU) 2024/573 should be taken into account, or only the period starting with the year in which the undertaking lawfully placed hydrofluorocarbons on the market for the first time as reported under Article 19 of Regulation (EU) No 517/2014, whichever event occurred first. This rule should not apply to undertakings that have placed hydrofluorocarbons on the market on the basis of quota that included quota transferred to them in accordance with Article 18 of Regulation (EU) No 517/2014. Instead, first, the rule should be that for each transfer in a given year, the starting year for the annual average should be the year in which the undertaking transferring their quota to the undertaking receiving the quota has entered the market (namely the year for which the undertaking making the transfer was allocated quota for the first time or the year in which that undertaking lawfully placed hydrofluorocarbons on the market for the first time, whichever event occurred first). That starting year may be 2015 or later depending on each case. Secondly, that rule should only be applied to the part of the placing on the market relating to quota transferred to the concerned undertaking. The part of the placing on the market relating to quota allocated to the concerned undertaking in a given year in accordance with Article 17(4) of Regulation (EU) 2024/573 should fall under the general rule. (6) In addition, in accordance with point 4(b) of Annex VII to Regulation (EU) 2024/573, a reference value should be calculated for each producer and importer that has reported on the placing on the Union market of hydrofluorocarbons for being used for the production of metered dose inhalers for the delivery of pharmaceutical ingredients. That reference value should be calculated on the basis of the annual average of the quantities of hydrofluorocarbons lawfully placed on the Union market for such usage, from 1 January 2020 as reported under Article 19 of Regulation (EU) No 517/2014 and under Article 26 of Regulation (EU) 2024/573, for the years available, on the basis of available data. Accordingly, for calculating the reference values referred to in point 4(b) of Annex VII to Regulation (EU) 2024/573 for the period from 1 January 2025 to 31 December 2026, any data reported by producers and importers in accordance with Article 19 of Regulation (EU) No 517/2014 for 2020, 2021, 2022 and 2023 should be taken into account. (7) For the purpose of identifying the starting year for the annual average calculation for the reference values referred to in point 4(b) of Annex VII to Regulation (EU) 2024/573, only the period starting with the year for which quantities for the placing on the market of hydrofluorocarbons for being used for the production of metered dose inhalers for the delivery of pharmaceutical ingredients reported for the first time in accordance with Article 19 of Regulation (EU) No 517/2014, should be taken into account. (8) In accordance with Article 18(3) of Regulation (EU) 2024/573, for the purpose of determining a reference value for each importer or producer pursuant to Article 17(1) of that Regulation and allocating quota for placing hydrofluorocarbons on the Union market pursuant to Article 17(4) of that Regulation, all undertakings with the same beneficial owner(s) should be considered as one single undertaking. Unless indicated otherwise by the beneficial owner(s), the single undertaking for which the sole reference value for all undertakings of the same beneficial owner(s) is established, should be the undertaking of the same beneficial owner(s) that was registered first in the F-gas Portal established pursuant to Article 20 of Regulation (EU) 2024/573. For this purpose, the sum of the individual quantities of hydrofluorocarbons lawfully placed on the Union market, calculated as per the annual average calculation for when the placing on the market includes quota transfers, for each undertaking of the same beneficial owner(s), should be taken into account when establishing a reference value of the importer or producer considered as the single undertaking of the same beneficial owner(s). (9) When determining reference values, consideration should also be made of a permanent succession or acquisition of the part of an undertaking’s business related to hydrofluorocarbons which have resulted in a change of the attribution of its reference values and those of its legal successor in accordance with Article 17(2) of Regulation (EU) 2024/573. Therefore, only the undertaking covered by Article 18(3) of Regulation (EU) 2024/573 that has been determined to being the legal successor should be entitled to a reference value. (10) Reference values for importers and producers should be determined on the basis of the information provided by the concerned undertakings in their individual profiles in the F-gas Portal by the time of the preparation of this Decision. (11) The measures provided for in this Decision are in accordance with the opinion of committee on fluorinated greenhouse gases established by Article 34 of Regulation (EU) 2024/573, HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION: Article 1 Reference values For the purpose of the quota allocation for the placing on the Union market of hydrofluorocarbons, the reference values for each importer and producer referred to in point 4(a) of Annex VII to Regulation (EU) 2024/573 shall be those set out in Annex I to this Decision, and the reference values for each importer and producer referred to in point 4(b) of Annex VII to Regulation (EU) 2024/573 shall be those set out in Annex II to this Decision. Article 2 Period of validity This Decision shall apply from 1 January 2025 and shall expire on 31 December 2026.
