(2) Pursuant to Article 17(1) of Regulation (EU) 2024/573, the Commission should determine reference values for each importer and producer that has lawfully placed on the Union market hydrofluorocarbons (HFCs) in 2021, 2022 or 2023, in accordance with the calculation mechanism referred to in points 4(a) and 4(b) of Annex VII to Regulation (EU) 2024/573. (3) As established by Annex VII to Regulation (EU) 2024/573, the maximum quantities of HFCs allowed to be placed on the Union market in 2025 and 2026 should be the same and are to decrease as from 2027.
