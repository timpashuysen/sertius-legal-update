TITLE: Corrigendum to Commission Regulation (EU) 2025/1988 of 2 October 2025 amending Annex XVII to Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances in firefighting foams (OJ L, 2025/1988, 3.10.2025)

L_202690757EN.000101.fmx.xml Official Journal of the European Union EN L series 2026/90757 8.9.2026 Corrigendum to Commission Regulation (EU) 2025/1988 of 2 October 2025 amending Annex XVII to Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances in firefighting foams ( Official Journal of the European Union L, 2025/1988, 3 October 2025 ) On page 9, in paragraph 2, point (a) of column 2 of the table in the Annex, amending Annex XVII to Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006: for: ‘perfluorooctane sulfonic acid (PFOS), its salts and PFOS-related compounds C 8 F 17 SO 3 X, to perfluorooctanoic acid (PFOA), its salts and PFOA-related compounds and to perfluorohexane sulfonic acid (PFHxS), its salts and PFHxS-related compounds, covered by Annex I to Regulation (EU) 2019/1021;’, read: ‘perfluorooctane sulfonic acid (PFOS), its salts and PFOS-related compounds C 8 F 17 SO 2 X, to perfluorooctanoic acid (PFOA), its salts and PFOA-related compounds and to perfluorohexane sulfonic acid (PFHxS), its salts and PFHxS-related compounds, covered by Annex I to Regulation (EU) 2019/1021;’. On page 9, in paragraph 2, point (b) of column 2 of the table in the Annex, amending Annex XVII to Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006: for: ‘linear and branched perfluorocarboxylic acids of the formula C n F 2n + 1-C(= O)OH where n = 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, or 13 (C9-C14 PFCAs) including their salts, and any combinations thereof, restricted under entry 68;’, read: ‘linear and branched perfluorocarboxylic acids of the formula C n F 2n+1 -C(= O)OH where n = 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, or 13 (C9-C14 PFCAs) including their salts, and any combinations thereof, restricted under entry 68;’.
