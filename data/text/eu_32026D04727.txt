TITLE: Commission Decision of 7 May 2026 instructing the Central Administrator of the Union Registry to enter corrections to the national allocation tables of Belgium, Ireland, Spain, France, Italy, Poland, Slovakia and Finland into the Union Registry

C_202604727EN.000101.fmx.xml Official Journal of the European Union EN C series C/2026/4727 2.9.2026 COMMISSION DECISION of 7 May 2026 instructing the Central Administrator of the Union Registry to enter corrections to the national allocation tables of Belgium, Ireland, Spain, France, Italy, Poland, Slovakia and Finland into the Union Registry (C/2026/4727) THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION, Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, Having regard to Directive 2003/87/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 October 2003 establishing a system for greenhouse gas emission allowance trading within the Union and amending Council Directive 96/61/EC ( 1 ) , Having regard to Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/1122 of 12 March 2019 supplementing Directive 2003/87/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the functioning of the Union Registry ( 2 ) , and in particular Article 47(3), second subparagraph, thereof, Whereas: (1) Member States submitted to the Commission the list of installations in their territory pursuant to Article 11(1) of Directive 2003/87/EC. (2) In accordance with Article 2 of Commission Decision (EU) 2021/355 ( 3 ) , the Commission raised no objections to the lists of installations covered by Directive 2003/87/EC as submitted by Belgium, Bulgaria, Czechia, Denmark, Germany, Estonia, Ireland, Greece, Spain, France, Croatia, Italy, Cyprus, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Hungary, the Netherlands, Austria, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovenia, Slovakia, Finland and Sweden, except as set out in Article 1 and Annexes of that Decision. (3) Member States determined and notified the preliminary annual amounts of free allowances per installation using the revised benchmark values determined in Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2021/447 ( 4 ) , pursuant to Article 14(5) of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/331 ( 5 ) . (4) In Article 1 of Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/927 ( 6 ) , it was determined that no adjustment needed to be applied to the preliminary annual amounts of free allowances pursuant to Article 10a(5) of Directive 2003/87/EC, as demand did not exceed the maximum amount. (5) Belgium notified changes to its national allocation table, by letter dated 4 July 2025. The electricity generator status has been corrected for installation BE-224. The non-ETS heat import of the nitric acid sub-installation has been corrected for installation BE-205758. The historical activity levels of the heat sub-installation and fuel sub-installation have been corrected for installation BE-205758. (6) Ireland notified changes to its national allocation table, by letter dated 17 February 2026. A dried secondary gypsum sub-installation has been added to installation IE-2. (7) Spain notified changes to its national allocation table, by letter dated 25 March 2026. The historical activity levels of the heat sub-installation and fuel sub-installation have been corrected for installation ES-519. The historical activity level of the carbon leakage heat sub-installation has been corrected for installation ES-758. A non-carbon leakage heat sub-installation has been added to installation ES-758. The historical activity level of the carbon leakage heat sub-installation has been corrected for installation ES-90021. A non-carbon leakage heat sub-installation has been added to installation ES-90021. The historical activity level of the carbon leakage heat sub-installation has been corrected for installation ES-90030. A non-carbon leakage heat sub-installation and a district heating sub-installation have been added to installation ES-90030. (8) France notified changes to its national allocation table, by letter dated 2 April 2026. A non-carbon leakage fuel sub-installation has been added to installation FR-55. The historical activity levels of the carbon leakage heat sub-installation and carbon leakage fuel sub-installation have been corrected for installation FR-55. The carbon leakage status of the process emissions sub-installation has been corrected for installation FR-204907. A non-carbon leakage fuel sub-installation and a non-carbon leakage process emissions sub-installation have been added to installation FR-204921. The historical activity levels of the carbon leakage fuel sub-installation and process emissions sub-installation have been corrected for installation FR-204921. The carbon leakage status of the heat sub-installation and fuel sub-installation have been corrected for installation FR-206303. A fuel sub-installation has been added to installation FR-206360. The process emissions sub-installation has been removed from installation FR-206360. (9) Italy notified changes to its national allocation table, by letter dated 7 April 2026. A heat sub-installation has been added to installation IT-352. The historical activity level of the heat sub-installation has been corrected for installation IT-941. A fuel sub-installation has been added to installation IT-941. (10) Poland notified changes to its national allocation table, by letters dated 15 January 2026, 19 January 2026 and 27 March 2026. The historical activity levels of the heat sub-installations have been corrected for installation PL-240. The historical activity level of the district heating sub-installation has been corrected for installation PL-456. The heat sub-installation has been removed from installation PL-456. The historical activity level of the carbon leakage fuel sub-installation has been corrected for installation PL-202254. The non-carbon leakage heat sub-installation, the non-carbon leakage fuel sub-installation and the non-carbon leakage process emissions sub-installation have been removed from installation PL-202254. A hydrogen sub-installation, a carbon leakage heat sub-installation and a carbon leakage process emissions sub-installation have been added to installation PL-202254. (11) Slovakia notified changes to its national allocation table, by letter dated 31 March 2026. The historical activity level of the lime sub-installation has been corrected for installation SK-17. The historical activity levels of the heat sub-installation and fuel sub-installation have been corrected for installations SK-118. The historical activity level of the fuel sub-installation has been corrected for installation SK-176. A heat sub-installation has been added to installation SK-176. The historical activity levels of the heat sub-installation and fuel sub-installation have been corrected for installation SK-197. (12) Finland notified changes to its national allocation table, by letter dated 31 March 2026. The historical activity levels of the heat sub-installation and district heating sub-installation have been corrected for installation FI-239. The process emissions sub-installation has been removed from installation FI-395. The historical activity level of the carbon leakage heat sub-installation has been corrected for installation FI-396. A non-carbon leakage heat sub-installation has been added to installation FI-396. The historical activity levels of the heat sub-installation and district heating sub-installation have been corrected for installation FI-397. The uncoated carton board sub-installation has been removed from installation FI-397. A fuel sub-installation has been added to installation FI-397. The historical activity levels of the heat sub-installations have been corrected for installation FI-399. The historical activity level of the tissue sub-installation has been corrected for installation FI-404. The lime sub-installation has been removed from installation FI-406. The carbon leakage heat sub-installation has been removed from installation FI-435. The historical activity level of the non-carbon leakage heat sub-installation has been corrected for installation FI-435. The historical activity levels of the uncoated fine paper sub-installation and coated fine paper sub-installation have been corrected for installation FI-471. The historical activity levels of the heat sub-installation, district heating sub-installation, fuel sub-installation and process emissions sub-installation have been corrected for installation FI-476. The non-ETS heat import and the electricity exchangeability factor of the synthesis gas sub-installation have been corrected for installation FI-476. The historical activity level of the process emissions sub-installation has been corrected for installation FI-610. The process emissions sub-installation has been removed from installation FI-623. The electricity exchangeability factor of the steam cracking sub-installation has been corrected for installation FI-623. The historical activity level of the dried secondary gypsum sub-installation has been corrected for installation FI-205649. The historical activity level of the fuel sub-installation has been corrected for installation FI-206161. (13) The notified national allocation tables are in conformity with Article 10c of Directive 2003/87/EC, Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/331 and Decision (EU) 2021/355. (14) It is therefore appropriate to instruct the central administrator to enter those corrections into the Union Registry, HAS DECIDED AS FOLLOWS: Sole Article The central administrator of the Union Registry is hereby instructed to enter corrections to the national allocation tables of Belgium, Ireland, Spain, France, Italy, Poland, Slovakia and Finland with the final annual amounts for the transitional free allocation of emission allowances for the period from 2021 to 2025, as set out in the Annex, into the Union Registry.
