TITLE: Commission Decision of 5 July 2024 on instructing the central administrator of the Union Registry to enter the national aviation allocation tables for 2024 and 2025 into the Union Registry

C_202405383EN.000101.fmx.xml Official Journal of the European Union EN C series C/2024/5383 4.9.2024 COMMISSION DECISION of 5 July 2024 on instructing the central administrator of the Union Registry to enter the national aviation allocation tables for 2024 and 2025 into the Union Registry (C/2024/5383) THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION, Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, Having regard to Commission Regulation (EU) 2019/1122 of 12 March 2019 supplementing Directive 2003/87/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards the functioning of the Union Registry ( 1 ) , and in particular Article 49 thereof, Whereas: (1) Directive (EU) 2023/958 of the European Parliament and of the Council ( 2 ) amended Directive 2003/87/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council ( 3 ) and changed the method of allocation of allowances for aviation in the years 2024 and 2025. (2) In accordance with Article 3c of Directive 2003/87/EC, the Commission determined the total number of allowances to be allocated in respect of aircraft operators and published it in Commission Decision (EU) 2024/1797 ( 4 ) . The Commission also determined the quantity of free allocation which would have taken place in 2024 under the rules for free allocation in force prior to the amendments introduced by Directive (EU) 2023/958. (3) Accordingly, it is necessary to calculate the allocation of free allowances to aircraft operators according to the rules laid down in Article 3d of Directive 2003/87/EC. These rules require that free allowances should be allocated to aircraft operators proportionately to their share of verified emissions from aviation activities reported for 2023. Therefore, aircraft operators, that according to Eurocontrol data had emissions covered by the EU ETS in 2023, were taken into account in the calculation, even if this data was not yet available in the Union Registry at the time of adoption of this Decision. Emissions data available on 31 May 2024 was used for this purpose. (4) Belgium, Bulgaria, Czechia, Denmark, Germany, Estonia, Ireland, Greece, Spain, France, Croatia, Italy, Cyprus, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Hungary, Malta, Netherlands, Austria, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovenia, Slovakia, Finland and Sweden notified the national aviation allocation tables to the Commission. (5) The Commission considers that the national aviation allocation tables notified by Member States are in accordance with Article 28a of Directive 2003/87/EC. The central administrator should therefore be instructed to upload the national aviation allocation tables in the Union Registry, HAS DECIDED AS FOLLOWS: Sole Article The central administrator shall enter the national aviation allocation tables of Belgium, Bulgaria, Czechia, Denmark, Germany, Estonia, Ireland, Greece, Spain, France, Croatia, Italy, Cyprus, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Hungary, Malta, Netherlands, Austria, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovenia, Slovakia, Finland and Sweden for 2024 and 2025, as set out in the Annex, in the Union Registry.
