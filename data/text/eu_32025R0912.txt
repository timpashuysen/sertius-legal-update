TITLE: Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/912 of 19 May 2025 laying down rules for the application of Regulation (EU) 2024/1991 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards a uniform format for the national restoration plan

L_202500912EN.000101.fmx.xml Official Journal of the European Union EN L series 2025/912 20.5.2025 COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING REGULATION (EU) 2025/912 of 19 May 2025 laying down rules for the application of Regulation (EU) 2024/1991 of the European Parliament and of the Council as regards a uniform format for the national restoration plan (Text with EEA relevance) THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION, Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2024/1991 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 24 June 2024 on nature restoration and amending Regulation (EU) 2022/869 ( 1 ) , and in particular Article 15(7) thereof, Whereas: (1) Regulation (EU) 2024/1991 requires each Member State to prepare and submit a draft national restoration plan by 1 September 2026 and to identify for that purpose the restoration measures necessary to meet the restoration targets and fulfil the obligations set out in Articles 4 to 13 of that Regulation. The Commission is to establish a uniform format for those national restoration plans with the assistance of the European Environment Agency. (2) Article 15(1) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1991 provides that the national restoration plan is to cover the period up to 2050, with intermediate deadlines corresponding to the targets and obligations set out in Articles 4 to 13 of that Regulation. The uniform format should provide the necessary structure and fields for that content. (3) Article 15(2) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1991 provides that with regard to the period from 1 July 2032, Member States can choose to limit the national restoration plans to a strategic overview of the elements referred to in Article 15(3) and the contents referred to in paragraphs 4 and 5 of that Article. The same applies to the revised national restoration plans resulting from the review to be carried out by 30 June 2032 in accordance with Article 19(1) of that Regulation, with regard to the period from 1 July 2042.
