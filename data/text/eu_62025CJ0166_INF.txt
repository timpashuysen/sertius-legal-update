TITLE: Judgment of the Court (Sixth Chamber) of 9 July 2026.#European Commission v Portuguese Republic.#Infringement proceedings – Directive 2010/75/EU – Industrial emissions (integrated pollution prevention and control) – Incorrect or incomplete transposition.#Case C-166/25.

62025CJ0166_INF Judgment of the Court (Sixth Chamber) of 9 July 2026 – Commission v Portugal (Industrial emissions) (Case C‑166/25) (Infringement proceedings – Directive 2010/75/EU – Industrial emissions (integrated pollution prevention and control) – Incorrect or incomplete transposition) 1. Actions for failure to fulfil obligations – Examination of the merits by the Court – Situation to be taken into consideration – Situation on expiry of the period laid down in the reasoned opinion (Art. 258 TFEU) (see paragraph 39) 2. Member States – Obligations – Implementation of directives – Failure to fulfil obligations – Adoption of amending directives pleaded as justification – Not permissible (Art. 258 TFEU) (see paragraph 40) 3. Member States – Obligations – Implementation of directives – Failure to fulfil obligations – National system pleaded as justification – Not permissible (Art. 258 TFEU) (see paragraph 41) 4. Environment – Integrated pollution prevention and control – Directive 2010/75 – Obligation to transpose the concept of hazardous substances into national law – Scope – Adoption by the Member State of an interpretative note pending the adoption of transposing measures designed to ensure the application of that definition – Failure to adopt binding measures – Failure to fulfil obligations (Art. 258 TFEU; European Parliament and Council Directive 2010/75, Art. 3(18)) (see paragraphs 45-48) 5. Environment – Integrated pollution prevention and control – Directive 2010/75 – Obligations of the Member States – Introduction of certain obligations for operators of a facility – Obligation on operators to inform the competent authorities immediately in the event of an accident having a significant impact on the environment and in the event of a breach of the conditions of the permit – Scope – National legislation requiring notification within forty-eight hours – Failure to fulfil obligations – Introduction of an administrative procedure designed to ensure that information is provided immediately – Irrelevant (Art. 258 TFEU; European Parliament and Council Directive 2010/75, Arts 7(a) and 8(2)(a)) (see paragraphs 51-54) 6. Environment – Integrated pollution prevention and control – Directive 2010/75 – Obligations of the Member States – Introduction of certain obligations incumbent upon the competent authority – Obligation on the competent authority to require operators of a facility to take any appropriate additional measures to limit the environmental consequences in the event of an incident or accident – Scope – National legislation which does not impose a subsequent obligation on the competent authority to adopt and implement itself the additional measures necessary to limit the consequences for human health or the environment of such incidents or accidents – National legislation referring solely to the operator’s obligation to take such additional measures as the competent authority deems necessary – No failure to fulfil obligations (Art. 258 TFEU; European Parliament and Council Directive 2010/75, Arts 7(c) and 8(2)(c)) (see paragraphs 57-61) 7. Environment – Integrated pollution prevention and control – Directive 2010/75 – Obligations of the Member States – Obligation to ensure, where general binding rules are adopted, an integrated approach and a high level of environmental protection, equivalent to that guaranteed by individual permit conditions – Infringement – Legislative amendment currently being prepared – Irrelevant (Art. 258 TFEU; European Parliament and Council Directive 2010/75, Art. 17(1)) (see paragraphs 64-67) 8. Environment – Integrated pollution prevention and control – Directive 2010/75 – Review of the permit conditions – Requirement for the competent authorities to make use of all information resulting from emission monitoring or inspections – Failure to transpose the said obligation – Failure to fulfil obligations – Legislative amendment currently being prepared – Irrelevant (Art. 258 TFEU; European Parliament and Council Directive 2010/75, Art. 21(2), second subpara.) (see paragraphs 70-73) 9. Environment – Integrated pollution prevention and control – Directive 2010/75 – Procedure for authorising an installation or activity likely to have transboundary effects – Obligation on the competent authorities to provide certain information to interested third Member States – Scope – National legislation stipulating the obligation to provide information exclusively regarding the outcome of the permit application and the grounds for the decision taken – Legislative amendment currently being prepared – Irrelevant (Art. 258 TFEU; European Parliament and Council Directive 2010/75, Art. 26(4)) (see paragraphs 76-81) 10. Environment – Integrated pollution prevention and control – Directive 2010/75 – Permit conditions applicable to plants incinerating or co-incinerating waste – Permit to be granted subject to compliance with the relevant requirements regarding the pH, temperature and flow of waste water discharges – National legislation which does not make the granting of such permit conditional upon compliance with those requirements – Failure to fulfil obligations – Legislative amendment currently being prepared – Irrelevant (Art. 258 TFEU; European Parliament and Council Directive 2010/75, Art. 45(1)(d)) (see paragraphs 85-88) 11. Environment – Integrated pollution prevention and control – Directive 2010/75 – Permit conditions applicable to waste incineration or co-incineration plants – Permit must cover the list of the various categories of hazardous waste that may be treated, their minimum and maximum mass flow rates, their minimum and maximum calorific values, and their maximum content of certain pollutants – National regulations do not specify the details that must be included in such a permit – Failure to fulfil obligations – Legislative amendment currently being prepared – Irrelevant (Art. 258 TFEU; European Parliament and Council Directive 2010/75, Art. 45(2)) (see paragraphs 91-94) 12. Environment – Integrated pollution prevention and control – Directive 2010/75 – Control of emissions – Obligations incumbent on operators of waste water arising from the cleaning of waste gases, treated collectively with other sources of waste water – Obligation to carry out the appropriate mass balance calculations in order to determine the emission levels attributable to waste water arising from the cleaning of waste gases, using the results of the measurements set out in an annex to Directive 2010/75– Scope – Failure to incorporate into national legislation all the measures set out in the said annex to the Directive – Failure to fulfil obligations – Legislative amendment currently being prepared – Irrelevant (Art. 258 TFEU; European Parliament and Council Directive 2010/75, Art. 46(4), second subpara.) (see paragraphs 97-103) 13. Environment – Integrated pollution prevention and control – Directive 2010/75 – Facilities and activities involving the use of organic solvents – Requirement to classify as ‘existing installations’ only those installations using organic solvents that were in operation on a specific date – National legislation setting out different dates, later than those laid down in the Directive, for the purposes of classifying such installations as existing – Failure to fulfil obligations – Legislative amendment currently being prepared – Irrelevant (Art. 258 TFEU; European Parliament and Council Directive 2010/75, Art. 57(1)) (see paragraphs 106-109) 14. Environment – Integrated pollution prevention and control – Directive 2010/75 – Permit conditions applicable to plants incinerating or co-incinerating waste – Compliance with the limit values for emissions into the air and water is subject to compliance with the conditions set out in an annex to that Directive – National regulations that do not refer to the conditions set out in that annex – Failure to fulfil obligations – Legislative amendment currently being prepared – Irrelevant (Art. 258 TFEU; European Parliament and Council Directive 2010/75, Art. 49, Annex VI, Part 8, point 1.3) (see paragraphs 112-117) Operative part 1. By failing, by the expiry of the period prescribed by the European Commission in its reasoned opinion of 28 September 2023, to transpose correctly or in full Article 3(18), Article 7(a), Article 8(2)(a), Article 17(1), the second subparagraph of Article 21(2), Article 26(4), Article 45(1)(d) and (2), the second subparagraph of Article 46(4) and Article 49, read in conjunction with point 1.3 of Part 8 of Annex VI and Article 57(1) of Directive 2010/75/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 24 November 2010 on industrial emissions (integrated pollution prevention and control), the Portuguese Republic has failed to fulfil its obligations under those provisions. 2. The Portuguese Republic is ordered to bear, in addition to its own costs, those incurred by the European Commission.
