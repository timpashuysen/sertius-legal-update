TITLE: Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/2326 of 13 October 2026 amending Implementing Decision (EU) 2018/1135 as regards simplification and update of the reporting requirements (notified under document C(2026) 7611)

L_202602326EN.000101.fmx.xml Official Journal of the European Union EN L series 2026/2326 20.10.2026 COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING DECISION (EU) 2026/2326 of 13 October 2026 amending Implementing Decision (EU) 2018/1135 as regards simplification and update of the reporting requirements (notified under document C(2026) 7611) (Text with EEA relevance) THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION, Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, Having regard to Directive 2010/75/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 24 November 2010 on industrial and livestock rearing emissions (integrated pollution prevention and control) ( 1 ) , and in particular Article 72(2) thereof, Whereas: (1) Article 72(1) of Directive 2010/75/EU requires Member States to report, in an electronic format, information on the implementation of that Directive, on representative data on emissions and other forms of pollution, on emission limit values, on the application of best available techniques (BAT), and on progress made concerning the development and application of emerging techniques. (2) Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2018/1135 ( 2 ) sets out the type, format and frequency of information relating to the years from 2017 that are to be made available by Member States for the purposes of reporting under Article 72(1) of Directive 2010/75/EU. In accordance with Article 1 of Implementing Decision (EU) 2018/1135, Member States are to make the information available using the harmonised electronic reporting tool provided by the Commission. (3) Pursuant to Article 72(2) of Directive 2010/75/EU, Implementing Decision (EU) 2018/1135 is to be updated whenever necessary and no later than 5 August 2026. HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION: Article 1 Implementing Decision (EU) 2018/1135 is amended as follows: 1. Article 1 is replaced by the following: ‘Article 1 1. For installations covered by Chapters II, III, IV and VIa of Directive 2010/75/EU, with the exception of waste incineration plants and waste co-incineration plants with a nominal capacity of less than 2 tonnes per hour, Member States shall make available to the Commission the information set out in Annex I using the format laid down in that Annex, for each installation covered. 2. For installations covered by Chapter V of Directive 2010/75/EU, Member States shall make available to the Commission the information set out in Annex II using the format laid down in that Annex. 3. Member States shall report the information set out in Annexes I and II using the harmonised electronic reporting tool made available by the Commission. 4. With regard to installations rearing poultry and pigs, this Article shall apply as follows: (a) from 1 January 2031 for installations covered by Chapter VIa of Directive 2010/75/EU with a capacity of 600 livestock units (LSU), or more; (b) from 1 January 2032 for installations covered by Chapter VIa of Directive 2010/75/EU with a capacity of 400 LSU or more; (c) from 1 January 2033, to all other installations covered by Annex Ia of Directive 2010/75/EU. 5. Until the dates referred to in paragraph 4, the reporting obligations set out in this Decision in the version applicable on 31 December 2027 shall apply to the installations referred to in that paragraph which fall within the scope of Directive 2010/75/EU in the version in force on 3 August 2024.’ ; 2. Article 2 is amended as follows: (a) the first paragraph is amended as follows: (i) ‘2017’ is replaced by ‘2027’; (ii) ‘30 June 2019’ is replaced by ‘30 September 2028’; (b) the second paragraph is amended as follows: (i) ‘reporting years 2017 and 2018’ is replaced by ‘reporting year 2027’; (ii) ‘For those reporting years’ is replaced by ‘For this reporting year’; (iii) ‘30 September 2019’ is replaced by ‘30 September 2028’; 3. Annex I is replaced by the text set out in Annex I to this Decision; 4. Annex II is replaced by the text set out in Annex II to this Decision. Article 2 This Decision shall apply from 1 January 2028. Article 3 This Decision is addressed to the Member States. Done at Brussels, 13 October 2026. ANNEX I Information on installations covered by Chapters II, III, IV and VIa of Directive 2010/75/EU [with the exception of waste incineration plants and waste co-incineration plants with a nominal capacity of less than 2 tonnes per hour] Note: Member States may indicate information that they consider to be confidential, giving the grounds that they consider preclude the Commission from making it publicly available. 1.1. Contextual information Type Format 1.1.1. Country identifier Identification of the country in which the reported installations and installation parts are located. 1.1.2. Reporting year Calendar year to which reporting refers. 1.2. Information on all installations covered by Chapter II of Directive 2010/75/EU Type Format 1.2.1. inspireId Unique identifier that complies with the requirements of Directive 2007/2/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council ( 1 ) , as further specified in Commission Regulation (EU) No 1089/2010 ( 2 ) , in particular point 8 of Annex IV to that Regulation. 1.2.2. thematicId (optional) Thematic object identifier. 1.2.3. Geometry Latitude and longitude (coordinates for the approximate centre of the installation) expressed with reference to the ETRS89 (2D)-EPSG:4258 coordinate reference system and with a precision of 5 decimal places. 1.2.4. Name of the installation Official denomination, proper name or conventional name of the installation. 1.2.5. Status The operational status of the installation. 1.2.6. Competent authority For activities covered by Annex I to Directive 2010/75/EU, the name of and contact information for: (a) the competent authority or authorities responsible for granting permits under Chapter II of that Directive; (b) the competent authority or authorities responsible for inspections under Chapter II of that Directive. 1.2.7. Activities carried out Identification of all activities covered by Annex I to Directive 2010/75/EU carried out at the installation, distinguishing between main and other activities. 1.2.8. BAT conclusions Identification of the Commission implementing decisions on BAT conclusions that are applicable to any of the activities carried out at the installation, distinguishing between main activity BAT conclusions and other applicable BAT conclusions. 1.2.9. Other relevant chapters in Directive 2010/75/EU Identification of the Chapters in Directive 2010/75/EU other than Chapter I and II that apply to the installation (or to parts thereof). 1.2.10. Baseline report submitted An indication of whether a baseline report, as referred to in Article 22(2) of Directive 2010/75/EU, has been submitted to the competent authority. 1.2.11. Environmental management system (EMS) From reporting year 2030: installations that carry out the activities listed in points 1 to 4, 6.1 a and 6.1 b of Annex I to Directive 2010/75/EU must provide the information in points (a) to (c), below From reporting year 2033: installations that carry out any other activities listed in Annex I to Directive 2010/75/EU must provide the information in points (a) to (c), below. a) Whether the installation is covered by an EMS. b) URL(s) at which the publicly available information in the EMS can be found (Article 14a(4) of Directive 2010/75/EU). c) Whether the EMS is in accordance with Regulation (EC) No 1221/2009 of the European Parliament and of the Council ( 3 ) , standard ISO 14001, BAT conclusions or another standard. 1.2.12. Permit granted (Article 5) and severe cases of non-compliance (Article 8(3) and (4)) (a) URL(s) at which the permit(s) is/are made publicly available. (b) An indication of severe cases of non-compliance with permit conditions (Article 8(3) and 4) of Directive 2010/75/EU) leading to the suspension of operations at the installation during the reporting year, and whether enforcement action has been taken in this regard. 1.2.13. Update of permit conditions (Article 21(3)) (a) An indication of whether permit conditions have been updated in accordance with Article 21(3) of Directive 2010/75/EU. (b) If applicable, the date on which the permit conditions were updated in accordance with Article 21(3) of Directive 2010/75/EU. 1.2.14. BAT derogation under Article 15(5) – Emission levels For installations for which permit conditions have been updated in accordance with Article 21(3) of Directive 2010/75/EU, an indication of whether a derogation has been granted under Article 15(5) of that Directive.
