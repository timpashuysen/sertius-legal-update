TITLE: Judgment of the Court (Eighth Chamber) of 4 June 2026.#European Commission v Hellenic Republic.#Failure of a Member State to fulfil obligations – Environment – Directive 91/271/EEC – Urban waste water treatment – Article 3 – Collecting systems – Individual systems or other appropriate systems – Article 4 – Secondary or equivalent treatment – Article 5 – Sensitive areas – Article 7 – Coastal waters

62024CJ0841_INF Judgment of the Court (Eighth Chamber) of 4 June 2026 – Commission v Greece (Urban waste water) (Case C‑841/24) (Failure of a Member State to fulfil obligations – Environment – Directive 91/271/EEC – Urban waste water treatment – Article 3 – Collecting systems – Individual systems or other appropriate systems – Article 4 – Secondary or equivalent treatment – Article 5 – Sensitive areas – Article 7 – Coastal waters – Article 10 – Waste-water treatment plants) 1. Environment – Urban waste-water treatment – Directive 91/271 – No installation of urban waste water collection systems – No justification drawn from environmental or financial reasons – Consequence – Infringement of the obligation to equip agglomerations of more than 2000 population equivalent with collection systems (Council Directive 91/271, Art. 3(1)) (see paragraphs 33-36, 38-40) 2. Actions for failure to fulfil obligations – Examination of the merits by the Court – Situation to be taken into consideration – Situation on expiry of the period laid down in the reasoned opinion (Art. 258 TFEU) (see paragraph 37) 3. Environment – Urban waste-water treatment – Directive 91/271 – No installation of urban waste water collection systems – No justification drawn from environmental or financial reasons – Consequence – Infringement of requirements concerning the design, construction, operation and maintenance of water treatment plants (Council Directive 91/271, Arts 3(1), 4(1) and 10) (see paragraphs 54-60, 65) 4. Environment – Urban waste-water treatment – Directive 91/271 – No installation of urban waste water collection systems – Consequence – Infringement of the obligation to subject to more stringent treatment discharges in sensitive areas (Council Directive 91/271, Arts 3, 5(2) and 10) (see paragraphs 70-76) 5. Environment – Urban waste-water treatment – Directive 91/271 – No installation of urban waste water collection systems – No justification drawn from environmental or financial reasons – Consequence – Infringement of the obligation to subject to appropriate treatment in the event of discharge of urban waste water into coastal waters by agglomerations of less than 10000 population equivalent (Council Directive 91/271, Arts 3(1), 7 and 10) (see paragraphs 83-88) Operative part The Court: 1.
