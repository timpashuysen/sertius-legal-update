of wild fauna and flora by regulating trade therein – Japanese seahorse – Regulation (EC) No 338/97 – Article 2(t) – Concept of ‘specimen’ – Article 4(2) – Import permit – Article 2(j) and Article Introduction of personal or household effects into the European Union) Environment – International trade in endangered species of wild fauna and flora – Regulation No 338/97 – Exemptions from the prohibition of trade – Species listed in Annex B to Regulation No 338/97 – Specimen – Concept – Preparation of a complex composition which contains extract of Japanese seahorse – Included – Whether the preparation in question can be classified as a medicinal product – No effect (Council Regulation No 338/97, Art. 2(t)) (see paragraphs 24-37, operative part 1) Questions referred for a preliminary ruling – Jurisdiction of the Court of Justice – Identification of the relevant aspects of EU law – Reformulation of the questions (Art. 267 TFEU) (see paragraphs 38, 39) Environment – International trade in endangered species of wild fauna and flora – Species listed in Annex B to Regulation No 338/97 – Introduction into the European Union – Import permit – Derogation – Personal or household effects – Criteria – Product in question used for therapeutic needs and not for commercial purposes – Introduction of a preparation containing extract of a species listed in Annex B, lawfully purchased in a third country and introduced into the European Union for the therapeutic needs of a private individual and not for commercial purposes – Introduction not subject to the obligation to present an import permit or, where applicable, a (re-)export document – Conditions (Council Regulation No 338/97, Arts 2(j) and 7(3) and Annex B; Commission Regulation No 865/2006, Art. 57(1)) (see paragraphs 40-67, operative part 2) Environment – International trade in endangered species of wild fauna and flora – Species listed in Annex B to Regulation No 338/97 – Introduction into the European Union without an import permit or a (re-)export document – Sanctions – National legislation which provides for the imposition of criminal sanctions for the introduction of specimens without an import permit – Not permissible if specimens introduced come within the derogation from the obligation to present an import permit – Permissible if specimens introduced fall outside of that derogation – Conditions – Proportionality of the applicable criminal sanction (Council Regulation No 338/97, Art. 16; Commission Regulation No 865/2006) (see paragraphs 68-77, operative part 3) See the text of the decision.
