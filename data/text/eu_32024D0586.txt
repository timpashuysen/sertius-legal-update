TITLE: Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2024/586 of 16 February 2024 amending and correcting Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/2432 as regards the determination of reference values for certain producers and importers for the period from 1 January 2024 to 31 December 2024 (notified under document C(2024) 857)

L_202400586EN.000101.fmx.xml Official Journal of the European Union EN L series 2024/586 23.2.2024 COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING DECISION (EU) 2024/586 of 16 February 2024 amending and correcting Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/2432 as regards the determination of reference values for certain producers and importers for the period from 1 January 2024 to 31 December 2024 (notified under document C(2024) 857) (Only the Bulgarian, Czech, Croatian, Danish, Dutch, English, Estonian, French, German, Greek, Hungarian, Irish, Italian, Latvian, Lithuanian, Maltese, Polish, Romanian, Slovenian and Spanish texts are authentic) (Text with EEA relevance) THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION, Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, Having regard to Regulation (EU) No 517/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 16 April 2014 on fluorinated greenhouse gases and repealing Regulation (EC) No 842/2006 ( 1 ) , and in particular Article 16(3) thereof, Whereas: (1) Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/2432 ( 2 ) determine reference values for the period from 1 January 2024 to 31 December 2024 for each producer and importer that has lawfully placed hydrofluorocarbons on the market for the relevant reference period as reported under Article 19 of Regulation (EU) No 517/2014. (2) Reference value for each producer and importer is to be calculated on the basis of the annual average of the quantities of hydrofluorocarbons lawfully placed on the market from 1 January 2015 for the years available, as reported under Article 19 of Regulation (EU) No 517/2014, while excluding quantities of hydrofluorocarbons for the usage referred to in Article 15(2) of that Regulation during the same period, on the basis of available data. (3) For the purpose of identifying the starting year for the annual average calculation, the Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/2432 takes into account whichever event occurred first: i) the year for which quota had been allocated to an undertaking for the first time in accordance with Article 16(5) of Regulation (EU) No 517/2014 or ii) the year in which the undertaking lawfully placed hydrofluorocarbons on the market for the first time, as reported under Article 19 of that Regulation. (4) This rule is not applied for a certain category of undertakings (‘the concerned undertakings’), in particular, for undertakings that have placed hydrofluorocarbons on the market on the basis of quota that included quota transferred to them in accordance with Article 18(1) of Regulation (EU) No 517/2014 and the sum of those quota transferred in all years starting with 2015 was equal or above the 100 tonnes of CO 2 equivalent limit set out in Article 15(2) of that Regulation. For the concerned undertakings, the period starting from 2015 was taken into account in all cases. The rationale, as stated in the Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/2432, was that the transferred quota was originally carried out on the basis of placing on the market in earlier years. (5) The methodology applied for the determination of reference values for the period 1 January 2024 to 31 December 2024 for the concerned undertakings should be amended. While the same methodology has been applied already in the Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2020/1604 ( 3 ) for the determination of reference values for the period 1 January 2021 to December 2023 for the concerned undertakings, it is as from 2024 onwards no longer appropriate to apply this methodology for the recalculation of their reference values because it no longer reflects the current market dynamics between undertakings that entered the market of hydrofluorocarbons later than 2015. (6) It is necessary to amend the reference values determined by Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/2432 with regard to two aspects: first, for each transfer in a given year, the starting year for the annual average should be the year in which the undertaking transferring their quota to the concerned undertaking has entered the market (namely the year for which the undertaking making the transfer was allocated quota or the year in which that undertaking lawfully placed hydrofluorocarbons on the market for the first time, whichever event occurred first). That starting year may be 2015 or later depending on each case. Secondly, that rule should only be applied to the part of the placing on the market relating to quota transferred to the concerned undertaking. The part of the placing on the market relating to quota allocated to the concerned undertaking in a given year in accordance with Article 16(5) of Regulation (EU) No 517/2014 should fall under the general rule. (7) It is also necessary to correct Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/2432 concerning a third aspect. One of the addressees listed in Article 3 of Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/2432 received a reference value calculated by erroneously applying Article 7(1) of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/661 ( 4 ) . Therefore, that reference value needs to be split and determined separately for two undertakings. An additional undertaking should therefore be added to the list in the Annex to Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/2432. (8) In this respect, the reference values for concerned undertakings for the period from 1 January 2024 to 31 December 2024 should be recalculated. That recalculation only affects 153 producers and importers, which will be notified separately of the respective reference value. The producers and importers whose reference values are not affected by this Decision, will not receive an individual notification of their unchanged reference value. (9) Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/2432 should therefore be amended accordingly. (10) Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/2432 applies from 1 January 2024. The amendments and corrections introduced with this Decision should therefore apply from the same date.
